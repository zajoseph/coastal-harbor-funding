**Re: Freedom of Information Act request — All Boating Infrastructure Grant Tier 2 (and Tier 1 project) proposals received, FY2010 to present**

Dear FOIA Officer:

Under the Freedom of Information Act, 5 U.S.C. § 552, and the Department of the Interior's FOIA regulations at 43 C.F.R. part 2, I request the following records.

## Records requested

Please treat this as a request for "all FWS records" of the kind described, and forward it to the Wildlife and Sport Fish Restoration program and any regional offices that hold them.

For every **BIG Tier 2** (nationally competitive) proposal submitted to FWS from **FY2010 to the present**, whether or not funded, and for every BIG Tier 1 project proposal included in state applications in the same period, a list with:

1. Applicant (lead applicant) legal name
2. UEI and/or DUNS number, if held
3. Applicant type (e.g. state, county, city, port district or other special district, tribe, nonprofit)
4. Applicant location: city, county and state (and project location if recorded separately)
5. Project or application title
6. Amount of federal funding requested (and total project cost, if recorded)
7. Fiscal year and funding round/competition (and NOFO number, if recorded)
8. Outcome: selected/awarded, not selected, determined ineligible, or withdrawn (and award amount if selected)
9. Any internal application identifier, so records can be matched across releases
10. Submitting state agency, and the subrecipient (marina or facility owner) if different from the state, with the facility name
11. National ranking score, if recorded

FWS publishes only funded Tier 2 project summaries and funding by state, so the unfunded proposals are what this request is mainly for.

## Format

Please provide the records electronically, in a machine-readable format (CSV or Excel; a database export or report from the grants management system is fine), as 5 U.S.C. § 552(a)(3)(B) provides when records are readily reproducible in that form. I am asking for a **list or data extract of the fields above, not copies of the applications themselves.** Producing an extract from an existing electronic database by running a query or report is a search, not the creation of a new record.

If a field is not held, please release the remaining fields rather than delaying the response. If any field or record is withheld, please release all reasonably segregable portions and cite the specific exemption for each withholding. If a subrecipient is a private marina, its name and requested amount are part of a public grant competition and not confidential commercial information; please redact only genuinely confidential business details. I am happy to receive records on a rolling basis.

## Fee category, fee waiver and fee limit

I am an independent researcher, so I fall in the "all other requesters" fee category. I understand this entitles me to the first two hours of search time and the first 100 pages (or equivalent) free.

I request a waiver of all fees under 5 U.S.C. § 552(a)(4)(A)(iii) and 43 C.F.R. part 2, because disclosure is in the public interest and is not primarily in my commercial interest:

- **Subject:** the records concern identifiable operations of the federal government: how FWS receives and selects applications for competitive grants under the Boating Infrastructure Grant program (ALN 15.622).
- **Informative value:** FWS publishes funded Tier 2 projects only; unfunded proposals and the facilities behind them are not public.
- **Contribution to public understanding:** I am building an open, documented dataset of federal harbor and coastal infrastructure funding, which tests whether competitive funding follows community need or capacity to apply. I will publish the cleaned data and documentation in a public repository under a Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY 4.0) license, archive a release with a DOI, and report the findings in a research paper and a short public policy brief. The data will be usable by researchers, journalists, ports and local governments.
- **Significance:** knowing who applied, not only who won, is what makes it possible to tell whether low-capacity communities are absent from these programs or are applying and losing. Award-only data cannot answer that question.
- **No commercial interest:** I have no commercial interest in the records and will not sell them or use them for any commercial purpose.

If the fee waiver is denied, I agree to pay fees up to **$25**. Please contact me before incurring any fees above that amount.
