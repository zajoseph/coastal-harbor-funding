Dear FOIA Officer:

Under the Freedom of Information Act, 5 U.S.C. § 552, and the Department of Transportation's FOIA regulations at 49 C.F.R. part 7, I request the following records.

## Records requested

DOT already publishes lists of FASTLANE and INFRA applications, and I am **not** asking for the information those lists contain. I already hold:

- **FY2016–FY2021:** DOT's "INFRA – Project Applications" PDFs (FASTLANE FY2016 and FY2017; INFRA FY2017–18, FY2019, FY2020 and FY2021), giving project name, applicant, state, amount requested and project cost for each application.
- **FY2022 onward:** DOT's INFRA application spreadsheets ("INFRA FY22 Applications.xlsx", "INFRA FY23-24 Applications.xlsx", "INFRA Applications 25-26.xlsx" and "INFRA FY26 Applications.xlsx"), giving applicant, state, urban/rural, amount requested, eligible cost and the award outcome and amount.

I request only the following fields, which those lists do not contain.

**Part A: FASTLANE (FY2016) and INFRA (FY2017 through FY2021).** For every application received:

1. Applicant UEI and/or DUNS number, if held
2. Applicant type (e.g. state, county, city, port district or other special district, tribe, nonprofit)
3. Applicant city and county (and project location, if recorded separately)
4. Outcome: selected/awarded, not selected, determined ineligible, or withdrawn (and award amount if selected)
5. Any internal or Grants.gov application identifier
6. For any application determined ineligible and left out of the published lists: applicant name, project title, state, round and amount requested, in addition to fields 1–5

**Part B: INFRA FY2022 onward.** For every INFRA application:

1. Applicant UEI and/or DUNS number, if held
2. Applicant type
3. Applicant city and county (and project location, if recorded separately)
4. FY2023 through FY2025 rounds only: any internal or Grants.gov application identifier (the FY2022 and FY2026 lists already show the Grants.gov tracking number)
5. FY2023 through FY2025 rounds only: applications determined ineligible (applicant name, project title, state, round and amount requested, plus fields 1–3). The FY2022 and FY2026 lists already include ineligible applications

Please exclude Mega and Rural applications unless the application was also considered for INFRA. I do not need outcome or award fields for FY2022 onward, as these are already public.

**Matching.** So that the records can be matched to the published lists, please include with each record the applicant name, project title, state and round as they appear in DOT's records. These are already public and are requested only as keys.

## Format

Please provide the records electronically, in a machine-readable format (CSV or Excel; a database export or report from the grants management system is fine), as 5 U.S.C. § 552(a)(3)(B) provides when records are readily reproducible in that form. I am asking for a **list or data extract of the fields above, not copies of the applications themselves.** Producing an extract from an existing electronic database by running a query or report is a search, not the creation of a new record.

If a field is not held, please release the remaining fields rather than delaying the response. If any field or record is withheld, please release all reasonably segregable portions and cite the specific exemption for each withholding. DOT already publishes applicant names, project titles, states and requested amounts for every FASTLANE and INFRA round since FY2016, and award outcomes from FY2022, so identifying applicants by type, location and registration number discloses nothing confidential. I am happy to receive records on a rolling basis.

## Fee category, fee waiver and fee limit

I am an independent researcher, so I fall in the "all other requesters" fee category. I understand this entitles me to the first two hours of search time and the first 100 pages (or equivalent) free.

I request a waiver of all fees under 5 U.S.C. § 552(a)(4)(A)(iii) and 49 C.F.R. part 7, because disclosure is in the public interest and is not primarily in my commercial interest:

- **Subject:** the records concern identifiable operations of the federal government: how DOT receives and selects applications for competitive grants under the FASTLANE and INFRA (Nationally Significant Multimodal Freight and Highway Projects) grant programs.
- **Informative value:** DOT's published lists name applicants but carry no registration number (UEI/DUNS), applicant type or city/county, and the FY2016–21 lists show no outcomes. Without these fields applicants can't be reliably linked to other federal funding records or classified by community, so the published lists can't show who applied and lost.
- **Contribution to public understanding:** I am building an open, documented dataset of federal harbor and coastal infrastructure funding, which tests whether competitive funding follows community need or capacity to apply. I will publish the cleaned data and documentation in a public repository under a Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY 4.0) license, archive a release with a DOI, and report the findings in a research paper and a short public policy brief. The data will be usable by researchers, journalists, ports and local governments.
- **Significance:** knowing who applied, not only who won, is what makes it possible to tell whether low-capacity communities are absent from these programs or are applying and losing. Award-only data cannot answer that question.
- **No commercial interest:** I have no commercial interest in the records and will not sell them or use them for any commercial purpose.

If the fee waiver is denied, I agree to pay fees up to **$25**. Please contact me before incurring any fees above that amount.
